# AI Infrastructure Co-Design

Research on progressive hardware–software co-design for AI infrastructure, with two connected areas:

- **[Design optimization](design-optimization/):** construct and solve workload-driven design problems spanning algorithmic organization, tensor programs, instruction interfaces, and hardware structure.
- **[Design verification](design-verification/):** establish that progressive design refinements preserve the required computation and reuse correctness evidence as designs evolve.

## Manuscripts

### Design optimization

[Agentic Progressive Optimization for Hardware–Software Co-Design of AI Infrastructure](design-optimization/papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/paper.pdf)

[LaTeX source](design-optimization/papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/paper.tex) · [Outline and build instructions](design-optimization/papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/README.md)

The manuscript develops the design-space formulation, hierarchical MIP construction, and agent-guided search method. Five vector figures and frozen empirical source records accompany the paper. The experiments analyze bounded Ventus co-design studies; the proposed framework is distinguished from the measured scope.
