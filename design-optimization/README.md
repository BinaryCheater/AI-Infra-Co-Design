# Design Optimization

Research on progressive construction and optimization of hardware–software design spaces for AI infrastructure.

## Manuscript

**Agentic Progressive Optimization for Hardware–Software Co-Design of AI Infrastructure**

- [Compiled PDF](papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/paper.pdf)
- [LaTeX source](papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/paper.tex)
- [Outline](papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/OUTLINE.md)
- [Build instructions and artifact description](papers/agentic_progressive_codesign/README.md)

The paper connects algorithmic and software choices, movable instruction interfaces, and hardware resources through MIP formulations. Its case studies examine preference reversals, joint and sequential optimization, and performance-model fidelity. This directory contains the manuscript and its source-data checks, not an implementation of the complete proposed agent framework.
